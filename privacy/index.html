Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how TaskedBuilds ("we," "our," or "the Bot") handles information when you interact with our Discord bots.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the bot's features, information processed may include Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, command inputs, server configuration settings, and technical error information. We only process information needed to provide and maintain the relevant features.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to respond to commands, provide bot functionality, maintain server settings, troubleshoot problems, protect against abuse, and maintain security.

4. Storage and Retention

Some information may be processed temporarily or stored to support bot features. The types of stored data and retention periods depend on each bot's implementation. Information should be retained only as reasonably necessary for its purpose or as required by law.

5. Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers needed to operate the bots or disclosed when required by law. Discord independently processes information under its own privacy policy.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information under our control. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your Privacy Requests

Where applicable, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your use of a bot by contacting its owner through the associated support server or published contact method. Some information may need to be retained for legal or legitimate operational reasons.

8. Children's Privacy

Our bots are not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. Please do not submit sensitive personal information through bot commands.

9. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated periodically. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or data-related requests, contact the Bot owner through the associated Discord support server or published contact method.
