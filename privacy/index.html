Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how TaskedBuilds ("we," "our," or "the Bot") handles information through our Discord ticket bot.

2. Information We Collect

The Bot processes ticket-related information submitted when users open or interact with support tickets. This may include ticket messages, ticket identifiers, and information needed to associate a ticket with the relevant Discord user or server.

We do not intentionally collect information unrelated to providing the ticket system. Please avoid submitting passwords, payment card details, or other sensitive information in tickets unless specifically necessary and handled through an appropriate secure process.

3. How Information Is Used

Ticket information is used to create, manage, and respond to support requests, maintain ticket functionality, and help authorized staff provide support.

4. Storage and Retention

Ticket information may be stored while a ticket is open or afterward if the Bot is configured to retain ticket transcripts or records. Retention depends on the Bot's actual configuration and the server's practices.

5. Sharing and Access

Ticket information may be visible to the user who created the ticket and authorized server staff. Information may also be processed by hosting or storage providers used to operate the Bot. We do not sell ticket information.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect ticket information. However, no electronic system can guarantee absolute security. Users should only share information necessary to resolve their support request.

7. Data Requests

For questions or requests concerning ticket information, contact the Discord server's administrators or the Bot owner through the associated support server. Requests will be handled where applicable and subject to legitimate operational or legal requirements.

8. Third-Party Services

The Bot operates through Discord, which processes information under its own Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

9. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the Bot owner through the associated Discord support server.
